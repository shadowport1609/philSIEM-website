PRIVACY & SCOPE

Privacy by design.

PhilSIEM is a static informational portfolio. It does not run live monitoring or accept user accounts.

Interactive lab

The simulated SOC exercise runs locally in your browser. Your notes are not submitted to a server. The optional report download is created on your device.

Hosting

This site is hosted using GitHub Pages. The hosting provider may process standard technical request data according to its own privacy practices. PhilSIEM does not independently operate analytics or advertising scripts in this package.

Public project data

Example addresses and domains in training scenarios are reserved for documentation or invalid examples. Architecture materials are illustrative and are not a live map of an exposed network.

Optional local training history

The interactive SOC lab stores saved assessments in your browser’s local storage only when you press “Save assessment on this device”. Saved data is not transmitted to PhilSIEM or any server. You can clear it from the lab or through your browser’s site-data settings. Exported reports are downloaded directly to your device. Avoid including personal data or sensitive logs in training notes.
