02 / INVESTIGATION MINDSET

From signal to evidence.

Security operations is not just spotting an alert. It is understanding the context, documenting findings and knowing what the evidence actually supports.

01

Observe

Review alerts, logs and relevant context.

02

Investigate

Identify patterns, scope and potential impact.

03

Document

Separate confirmed facts from hypotheses.

04

Improve

Refine detections and record lessons learned.

i

Evidence-first approach. Any public case studies will use sanitised lab data or clearly labelled simulations. Sensitive systems, credentials and personal information stay private.
